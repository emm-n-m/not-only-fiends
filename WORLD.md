The campaign revolves around DECEIT (Demonic Excellence Center for the Education of Infernal Temptresses) — an Abyssal-layer academy for succubi that didn't get the memo and operates more like an advanced magic research center than a prep school.
The setting is a spin-off of a creative writing project.
On the name
The repo takes its name from NOT-OnlyFiends, the in-universe crystal-ball platform run by Imp, Professor of Divination at DECEIT.
